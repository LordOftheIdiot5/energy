# Energy V11 release candidate

V6: weekly NVE hydro history with date-range selection, seeded from five actual published weeks. Existing collector appends new valid weeks to the same versioned archive, preserving first recorded observation.
V7: country-specific price/weather views, retaining bidding-zone units.
V8: transparent heating-demand scenario, not a trained forecast.
V9: filterable hydro explorer and CSV export with attribution.
V10: deterministic snapshot research assistant, not an LLM.
V11: static versioned hydro JSON and catalog. Commercial authentication, billing and SLAs are not implemented. Source-specific redistribution rights for mixed feeds remain unverified.

NVE source: https://api.nve.no/doc/magasinstatistikk/; license: https://data.norge.no/nlod/. Regional boundaries remain representative points. No third-party mixed-feed archive or resale is added. No credentials are exposed. No new workflow or schedule.
