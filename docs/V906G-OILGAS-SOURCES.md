# V90.6g — North American oil and gas historical facilities

Source: https://geoappext.nrcan.gc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/NACEI/energy_infrastructure_of_north_america_en/MapServer

The North American Cooperation on Energy Information (NACEI) says service reuse is free and unrestricted if the source is acknowledged. Point registers: natural gas processing plants layer 4, LNG terminals layer 5, refineries layer 10. Publisher: Natural Resources Canada with US and Mexican energy institutions. Copyright and reference period in layers: **August 2017**. The data must never be labeled current operational status or current ownership.

This first-stage audit contacts official GIS only and stores a review artifact. Strictly excluded: PHMSA NPMS restricted GIS (including pipeline centerlines and LNG plant GIS); a Data.gov public metadata page is **not** a GIS reuse permission.

No data reaches Nordl production until independent real-source QC, attribution, period-aware UI, and browser checks pass. Planned follow-up: GEM CC BY 4.0 global LNG/pipeline release research, with 2026 LNG source directly downloaded from rights holder when permissible rather than mislabeling 2025 exports as current.
