# History expansion
Backfill uses bounded resumable tasks: seven complete UTC days of four Finnish three-minute series and up to 365 days of daily sampled HydAPI series. Twelve requests per provider per run; no overwrites of recent observations. Each successful request is tracked, including empty results; failed queries remain pending. Historical charts select monthly archives and join only consecutive observations. Exact REGINE matches are published as shared references, not upstream/downstream or plant inflow. Historical growth and real match counts must be verified after deployment.
