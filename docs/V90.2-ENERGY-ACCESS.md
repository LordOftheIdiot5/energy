# Energy access and differentiation
Three task-first links for electricity zones, infrastructure, and data downloads. Filter and preview published observations, export CSV/JSON with attribution and licence; archive endpoints remain free. Source-reported regulated-water transfer edges use NVE delfeltNr and nesteDelfeltNr exactly, documented by NVE product specification, not natural routing or measured plant flow. Ambiguous targets excluded. Additional eight active discharge stations rotate daily without dropping existing core or archived data. Full ELVIS natural-river routing, price/flow causal analysis, hosted authenticated API, paid subscriptions, and broader international data ingestion remain later work. No features removed.
