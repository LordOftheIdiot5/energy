Status: Owner selected C — Energy Network. Production SVG and responsive lockups implemented; acceptance subject to staged and live verification.

A distinctive vector mark and favicon are missing. Existing theme tokens will be retained, not replaced wholesale.

## Logo concepts for review
A — Current / monogram. Original angular N/current path, bold single symbol. Cyan and mint accent on navy; refers to electricity and a shared Nordl family.
B — Water to power. Two simplified hydraulic wave paths and a crisp negative-space energy stroke, emphasizing hydropower and physical energy. Avoid a generic recycled-energy leaf or a detailed turbine.
C — Energy network. Geometric connected-node mark and directional energy path, representing integrated grid/infrastructure intelligence. Avoid recreating Leivro's star or Stockwatch's compass.
All use a clean, readable NORDL ENERGY wordmark. No fine decorative lines, gradients required for recognition, or brand-dependent imagery. Mark must remain legible in a 16px favicon and 32–40px navigation context. Review concepts on dark and white before selecting. Design intent: premium research technology, not residential electricity supplier.

Reuse existing working theme values; cyan may be a secondary data colour, not a replacement for the price legend. Distinguish source data, forecasts, normal bands and scenarios by explicit legend, stroke style and marker shape as well as colour. Retain cheap-to-expensive colour mapping and avoid equating rising prices with good or bad.
Typography: retain Manrope (titles), DM Sans (interface), JetBrains Mono or tabular numerals for dense data. Use system fallback. No new external fonts or third-party brand assets.
Spacing grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 px; panel radius 10–14 px; 40–44 px targets on touch interfaces; keep existing accessible keyboard interaction and reduced-motion behavior.
Components: top context/date/zone controls; compact KPI with unit, source time and direction; map legend/filter/inspector; chart title, units, period, crosshair/readout, export; coverage/stale labels; data preview and source links. Reuse existing elements and selectors; preserve routes. Keep dense technical detail expandable. Navigation and future daily monitor should prioritize task discovery, not add decorative blocks.
Review: 1440, 1024, 390, 360 px; both themes; visible contrast, text legibility, focus, menu reachability, no horizontal page overflow; live data and map fallback after eventual integration.

## Approval gate
Owner chooses A, B or C (or requests revisions). Only then build production SVG mark and lockups, favicon, social preview and header integration; test staged artifact and actual production. V90.3b daily monitor follows the approved identity. No production logo or palette change is authorized by this draft alone.

## Selected production identity
Owner selected C on 19 November 2026. Original scalable vector interpretation of concept board, with six network vertices, blue connectors and a mint energy path on a navy tile. Assets in brand/: mark/favicon, dark/light wordmarks, SVG and PNG social artwork, theme-aware header CSS. Existing palette and navigation routes retained. Website headers, methodology and generated static asset/directory pages use this identity; favicon consistent across routes. Social PNG is a simplified original raster lockup for compatible link previews. No data or source changes.
