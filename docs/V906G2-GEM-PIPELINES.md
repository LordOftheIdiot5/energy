Rights holder: Global Energy Monitor, Global Gas Infrastructure Tracker. Underlying database CC BY 4.0, attribution preserved in distribution file and each row.
Official tracker: https://globalenergymonitor.org/projects/global-gas-infrastructure-tracker
Public normalized distribution: https://lngatlas.org/data/pipelines.json
Map routes: https://www.lngatlas.org/api/map/pipelines.json
**Release: November 2025 (4,246 records in distributor file)**. This is not 2026 current operations, and GEM's official totals may differ due project/segment normalization. Routes simplified ~2 km and may be approximate. Do not use as engineering-quality geography, and do not add capacities/lengths across statuses.

The source audit generates only counts, schema, identity, licences, status and route coverage and saves hashes. No live frontend, downloads, or promotion from this PR. Require manual source-review and documented transformation before republication.

2026 LNG terminals: latest GEM release October 2026; source uses its own formal download form. Do not mislabel September 2025 aggregator terminal data as the latest or bypass rights-holder access requirements.

PHMSA NPMS detailed pipeline GIS is excluded because public metadata does not imply redistribution approval.
