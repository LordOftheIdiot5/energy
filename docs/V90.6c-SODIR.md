# V90.6c offshore production
Official NOD FactMaps profiles 7300 monthly saleable production; NLOD 2.0 (https://www.sodir.no/en/facts/data-and-analyses/open-data/). Explicit source-field allowlist only, no third-party reports or images. Gas is billion Sm3, other fields million Sm3; no cross-unit aggregation. Source counts before and after pagination, stable field IDs and months, nulls retained, year partitions, prior versions saved separately. Current year refresh daily, one older missing year per day back to 1971; subject to actual source coverage. First live probe and production artifact tests required. Ownership and map geometry not yet implemented.
