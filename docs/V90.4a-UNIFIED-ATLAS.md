# V90.4a — Unified Energy Atlas foundation
Adds independent NVE power-station and HydAPI measurement-station overlays to the existing market atlas, retaining WRI historical plants, market observations, snow, physical grid, topographic and vector fallback. Shared source-aware asset search by name, ID, owner, place, technology, country and reported minimum capacity. Capacity filter excludes unknown or non-generating records when active. Distinct source IDs, no cross-source deduplication or operating-status inference. Record inspector and profile/source links; search remains usable when detailed map unavailable. New markers cluster in screen space and repaint after zoom/resize. Full polygon/transfer/river overlays and optional globe are later work; existing catchment and reservoir explorer remains accessible. Visual style of external basemap unchanged. No data rights or collector changes.
