Weather → water → energy

Catchment scenario. Enter your own basin-wide rainfall, available snow water equivalent (SWE), positive degree-days, and hydrological assumptions. Representative point precipitation above is not automatically substituted for catchment rainfall.

Water balance uses a simplified degree-day melt cap and effective runoff. It omits delayed routing, groundwater, changing snowpack, pumping and spill; inputs require local calibration before operational use.
