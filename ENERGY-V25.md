## Integration
Use https://energy.nordl.dev/data/hydro-v1.json for weekly NVE observations, preserving source/license/attribution and reporting week. Optional hydro-plants-v1.json and norway-zones-v1.json appear only after successful import. Treat HTTP error, missing schema, or old timestamps as unavailable, not zero. Existing energy.json is mixed-source and not commercially cleared. Stockwatch may display reservoir deviation as of observation week, not as a causal stock signal. Leivro needs verified tariff and consumer-price semantics, not wholesale price mislabeled as retail price. Forecast issue and target periods and revision history must be retained before building a commercial model. No external AI claims, no actual price or hydrological prediction yet.
